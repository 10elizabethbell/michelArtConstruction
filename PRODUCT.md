# PRODUCT — MichelArt Construction

## Platform
- Platform: web
- Stack: static single-file HTML/CSS/JS (index.html + img/), hosted on GitHub Pages
- Primary viewport: phone (opened from Facebook links, often in Facebook's in-app browser)

## Users
- North Jersey homeowners planning a roof job or a renovation (kitchen, bathroom, tile shower, drywall/paint). Found via Facebook group posts and Instagram.
- The owner, Mike, who receives the site first and must recognise his own business in it.

## Purpose
- Turn a curious homeowner into a text or call to Mike for a free estimate.
